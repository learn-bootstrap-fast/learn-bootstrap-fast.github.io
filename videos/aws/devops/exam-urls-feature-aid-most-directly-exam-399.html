The Blue-Green Deployments pattern is most facilitated by the 'Swap Environment URLs' feature in AWS Elastic Beanstalk. This capability allows you to smoothly direct traffic from one environment to another by switching their URLs, resulting in near-zero downtime during releases. It lets you fully validate your updated environment (the 'green') in isolation before making it live, while the previous production environment (the 'blue') remains unaffected until you are ready to switch.

The other options, such as Rolling Mutable Deployments and Immutable Rolling Updates, involve updating instances within the same environment and do not make extensive use of swapping environment URLs since they focus on gradual instance updates rather than outright traffic redirection. Canary Releases introduce new versions to a small portion of users incrementally, but do not inherently require swapping environment URLs since they use controlled rollout inside the same environment.

Tips: Use 'Swap Environment URLs' for deployments that need seamless cutovers, quick rollbacks, and comprehensive pre-launch testing, which are central to the Blue-Green approach.
