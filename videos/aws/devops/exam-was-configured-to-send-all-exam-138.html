The Leverage Amazon Kinesis Data Firehose to seamlessly deliver real-time streaming data from the Kinesis Stream to the Splunk environment option is the most suitable and efficient method for this scenario. Kinesis Data Firehose provides a fully managed and automatic way to capture, transform, and load data streams directly into Splunk with minimal configuration, reducing operational overhead and complexity.

The following explains the incorrect choices:

  • Configure the Kinesis Stream to automatically transmit incoming logs directly to Splunk for indexing: There is no built-in feature in Kinesis Streams to natively deliver data directly to Splunk without custom development or an additional service like Firehose.
