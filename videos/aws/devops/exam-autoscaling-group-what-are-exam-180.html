The correct answers are Pending:Wait and Terminating:Wait. These two states correspond to scaling out and scaling in, respectively. When a lifecycle hook is added, instances entering the Pending state (being launched) move to Pending:Wait before becoming active, allowing additional setup or configuration tasks. Likewise, instances being terminated enter Terminating:Wait, pausing termination for extra actions like log collection or graceful shutdown.

Exiting:Wait and Launching:Wait are incorrect, as there are no such wait states in the Auto Scaling lifecycle. It's crucial to remember that only Pending:Wait (for launches) and Terminating:Wait (for terminations) are controllable through lifecycle hooks.

Tip: Associate Pending:Wait with scale-out/setup, and Terminating:Wait with scale-in/cleanup, so you can easily recall which hooks apply to which process.
