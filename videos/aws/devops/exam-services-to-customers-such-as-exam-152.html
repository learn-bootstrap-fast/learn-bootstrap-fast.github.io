For a warm standby disaster recovery strategy, there must be rapid failover and near-real-time replication to meet the 30-minute RTO and RPO.

Auto Scaling Group helps automatically restore compute capacity by scaling EC2 instances quickly during a disaster, vital for hitting a 30-minute recovery target.

Route53 enables swift DNS failover to route users to the standby environment efficiently, achieving both quick recovery time and minimizing data loss.

The other options do not suit these DR requirements:

S3 Glacier is an archival storage solution with retrieve times ranging from minutes to hours, far too slow for a 30-minute RTO.

RDS MySQL Serverless capability is not the same as maintaining a consistent, warm replica for disaster recovery and does not guarantee the RPO/RTO requirements for warm standby.

AWS Storage Gateway is designed for hybrid cloud storage and backup, not for providing fast application and data restoration as needed here.

Tip: For warm standby, use AWS native features like Auto Scaling and Route53 with multi-AZ/multi-region replication to achieve strict RTO and RPO goals. Always match the DR pattern and corresponding AWS features to specific business objectives.
