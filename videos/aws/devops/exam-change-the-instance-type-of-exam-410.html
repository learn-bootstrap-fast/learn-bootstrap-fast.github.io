The correct choice is Perform a rolling update by configuring an Auto Scaling policy in CloudFormation to change instances in batches. By using the AutoScalingRollingUpdate policy within your CloudFormation template's update policy, you can perform rolling updates that terminate and replace instances in batches while maintaining a specified minimum number of healthy instances. This guarantees high availability and meets the requirement of always keeping at least two instances operational during the update.

The option Schedule an Auto Scaling action to update instance types at a specific time is incorrect because AutoScalingScheduledAction is meant for scaling activities (like increasing/decreasing the number of instances) at specific times, not for modifying the instance type as part of a CloudFormation update.

Replace all Auto Scaling instances simultaneously using the replacing update policy (AutoScalingReplacingUpdate) is not suitable, as it could potentially lead to downtime—this policy replaces all instances at once, violating the requirement to always have at least two instances running.

Use Auto Scaling integration update to refresh the environment is not a valid Auto Scaling update policy and does not apply in this scenario.

Tip: For production environments where availability must be maintained, always use a rolling update strategy and tune the parameters (like MinInstancesInService and MaxBatchSize) to preserve uptime.
