The Auto Scaling lifecycle hook option is the correct choice. By configuring a lifecycle hook, instances are paused in a Pending:Wait state while bootstrapping activities are performed. The option Increase the desired capacity in your Auto Scaling group settings to minimize the time needed for future bootstrapping of instances does not solve the primary issue—instances will still be marked In Service and monitored before they are prepared.

Tip: AWS Auto Scaling lifecycle hooks are purpose-built to pause instance transitions and ensure that setup tasks can complete before the instance is counted as fully active. Rely on these hooks for robust, maintainable, and scalable bootstrapping management.
