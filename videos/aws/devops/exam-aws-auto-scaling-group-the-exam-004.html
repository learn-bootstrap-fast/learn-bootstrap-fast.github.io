The scheduled scaling activity will execute instantly at 8:00 AM, regardless of the cooldown period in progress. This is because Auto Scaling scheduled actions do not wait for the cooldown period of previous scaling events before starting; instead, scheduled actions trigger at their specified time.

Option The scheduled scaling action will trigger at 8:45 AM, since scheduled actions consider the cooldown timer and will be delayed until the cooldown period completes is incorrect because scheduled actions don't wait for cooldown expiration; they start based on the defined schedule.

Option The scheduled scaling event will take place at 8:30 AM, waiting for the cooldown timer to finish is not right, since this assumes the scheduled event actually waits for the cooldown to end, which is not the case.

Option The scheduled scale-up won't proceed this time due to the running cooldown timer and will only resume the next day is also inaccurate, as cooldown timers do not prevent scheduled actions altogether.

Tip: Remember, cooldown periods delay subsequent scaling activities triggered by dynamic policies but do not postpone scheduled actions. Scheduled scaling always takes place at the appointed time, regardless of current cooldown status.
