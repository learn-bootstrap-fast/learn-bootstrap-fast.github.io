What are the target Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) for the application? and What is the approved budget allocation for the disaster recovery setup? are the most crucial questions to ask before designing a DR strategy.

Similarly, knowing the available budget helps you select a DR pattern that meets both business needs and financial constraints, as DR solutions can range from simple (lower RTO/RPO, less expensive) to advanced (minimal downtime/data loss, higher cost).
