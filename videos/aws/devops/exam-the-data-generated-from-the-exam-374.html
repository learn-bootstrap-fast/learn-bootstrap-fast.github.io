The Leverage AWS QuickSight to connect to your S3 and Redshift sources option is the most cost-effective and practical solution. QuickSight is a fully managed, scalable BI service that integrates seamlessly with AWS data sources like S3 and Redshift. It allows you to build and share interactive dashboards and includes advanced features such as machine learning insights. This keeps costs low since there's no need to maintain additional infrastructure or third-party software, and you only pay for what you use.

The Install open-source reporting tools like Tableau option is less suitable because Tableau is not free and can involve significant licensing costs, as well as ongoing setup and server management overhead, making it impractical with a limited budget.

With the Configure CloudWatch to capture metrics from S3 and Redshift approach, CloudWatch is primarily designed for infrastructure monitoring, not in-depth business intelligence, and does not natively support complex data analysis or rich data visualizations from S3 or Redshift data.

Using Implement Kinesis Data Firehose and Data Analytics to stream and process your data is focused on real-time data processing rather than interactive reporting and dashboards for historical analysis. Kinesis Dashboards are also far less powerful for business intelligence use cases compared to QuickSight.

Tip: For most analytics and dashboarding needs on AWS, QuickSight represents the most cost-effective and low-maintenance choice for teams needing to analyze and share insights from data stored in S3 or Redshift.
