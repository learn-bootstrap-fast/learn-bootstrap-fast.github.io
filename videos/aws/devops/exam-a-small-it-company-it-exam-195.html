  • Set up the ASG by utilizing an Auto Scaling Launch Configuration, adjusting the On-Demand and Spot capacity percentage settings, and then launch the ASG with this Launch Configuration.: This is inaccurate because support for mixing On-Demand and Spot capacity in Launch Configurations is very limited, and newer AWS features for mixed instance policies are only accessible via Launch Templates—not Launch Configurations.
