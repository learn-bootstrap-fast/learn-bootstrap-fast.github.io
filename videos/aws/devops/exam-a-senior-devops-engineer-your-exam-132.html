Deploy an Elastic Beanstalk environment with built-in auto-healing enabled and Set up an Auto Scaling Group attached to an Elastic Load Balancer are the best choices for ensuring high availability and automated healing in AWS.

  • Elastic Beanstalk natively supports health checks and auto-healing. When you enable these features, Beanstalk automatically replaces unhealthy instances—maintaining application availability with minimal manual intervention.
  • Auto Scaling Groups with an Elastic Load Balancer allow your platform to distribute incoming traffic among healthy instances, and automatically launch new instances if existing ones fail or demand rises. This self-healing and scaling capability guarantees seamless user experience and increased resilience.
  • Utilize a CloudFormation template to initiate infrastructure and application resources is incorrect: CloudFormation efficiently deploys and manages stacks, but it does not inherently provide auto-healing or high availability mechanisms—it simply provisions resources.
  • Establish a CloudWatch alarm on CPU usage to notify your operations team through SNS is not sufficient: While CloudWatch alarms help monitor and alert, this only provides notifications, not automated self-recovery or high availability.
  • Build an OpsWorks stack and enable auto-healing at the layer level is less common and more complex for general CI/CD solutions compared to ASG and Beanstalk. While OpsWorks offers auto-healing, it is typically used for Chef/Puppet-managed environments rather than typical application hosting.

Tip: Prefer managed services like Elastic Beanstalk for easier HA and self-healing out-of-the-box. Combine Load Balancers with Auto Scaling for core, proven AWS-native resilience and recovery.
