The most effective ways for the startup to reduce operational costs while establishing a disaster recovery solution are:

Employing an appropriate Auto Scaling Group to regulate the quantity of active server instances allows the company to automatically scale EC2 resources up or down based on demand. This ensures that, during low-usage or standby periods, the number of running instances is minimized, directly lowering costs. Using Auto Scaling also removes the manual overhead of managing capacity and ensures you pay only for the infrastructure currently required.

With leveraging a Trusted Advisor to identify low-utilization EC2 instances in standby environments and terminate unnecessary ones, the company can receive actionable insights for cost optimization. Trusted Advisor reviews resource utilization and provides recommendations to terminate or downsize idle or underutilized EC2 instances, which can save significant costs, especially in a disaster recovery setup that's rarely used.

Why are the other options not ideal?

Set up an S3 data lifecycle rule to transition all stored S3 data to Glacier after 24 hours to cut storage expenses. — While Lifecycle Policies help reduce storage costs, transferring all data to Glacier after just one day may not be practical for disaster recovery, as restoring data from Glacier is slow and could impact recovery time objectives.

Expand the number of spot instances in the hot standby infrastructure. — Increasing spot instances does not inherently minimize costs. Spot instances can be unreliable for disaster recovery because they might not be available when needed, and increasing their count actually raises costs.

Use Amazon Data Lifecycle Manager to remove outdated EBS snapshots automatically. — Although cleaning old EBS snapshots saves on storage, this does not address the primary ongoing expenses of compute and active storage required for a disaster recovery system.

Tip: Prioritize cost-control mechanisms that directly limit the number and utilization of active resources in standby to optimize your disaster recovery budget.
