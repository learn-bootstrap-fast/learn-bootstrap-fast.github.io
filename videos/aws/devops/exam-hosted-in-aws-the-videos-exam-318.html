The best option is to use Spot Instances in a launch configuration with the User Data script installing your custom video processing tool, deployed via an Auto Scaling group. Spot Instances are highly cost-effective, offering significant savings compared to On-Demand or Reserved Instances. Since the video processing app is fault-tolerant and can handle interruptions and recover gracefully, Spot Instances are appropriate even though they can be terminated at any time by AWS.

The options Reserved Instances and On-Demand Instances are more expensive and are suited for applications requiring guaranteed availability. Dedicated Instances are intended for isolated hardware requirements, further increasing costs and are unnecessary here. For this particular workload, where resilience to interruption exists and minimizing costs is the top priority, Spot Instances provide the optimal solution. Make sure your User Data automation installs necessary packages and dependencies on each instance launch, leveraging the Auto Scaling group for elasticity and management.

Tip: Always match your instance purchase option to the tolerance of your workload for cost savings. Use Spot for interruption-resilient stateless or recoverable workloads, and reserve or on-demand for critical, always-on services.
