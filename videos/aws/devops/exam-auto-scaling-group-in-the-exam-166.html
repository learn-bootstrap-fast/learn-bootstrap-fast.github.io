The correct way to move your instance from Wait to InService after your health check passes is by using the complete-lifecycle-action AWS CLI command (or its API equivalent). When you configure an Auto Scaling group with a lifecycle hook, it pauses instances at 'Pending:Wait' or 'Terminating:Wait' until you signal that your custom actions are done. complete-lifecycle-action is designed to let Auto Scaling know when you are finished, so it can resume normal processing.

Why the other options are incorrect:

  • Run 'record-lifecycle-action-heartbeat'... - This action merely extends the timeout of the lifecycle hook but does not complete the process or transition the instance out of the Wait state.
  • Employ the 'attach-instances'... - This command is for attaching standalone EC2 instances to an Auto Scaling group. It doesn't signal lifecycle hook completion and will not move the instance from Wait to InService in this context.
  • Use the 'put-lifecycle-hook'... - This command creates or updates lifecycle hooks; it does not manage the completion or continuation of an individual instance through the hook.

Tip: Always use complete-lifecycle-action in your automation to exit custom Wait states and resume Auto Scaling's operations. Remember: heartbeat commands buy you more time, but complete-lifecycle-action is the signal that you're done!
