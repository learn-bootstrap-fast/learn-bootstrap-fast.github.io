The optimal approach is to utilize Configure a scheduled Autoscaling policy to increase capacity every Monday morning and decrease capacity every Friday evening. Scheduled scaling allows you to precisely react to predictable workload changes, automating the scaling actions based on known times of increased and decreased demand. This method ensures the right amount of resources are available during peak times while saving costs during low periods.

Manually increase Autoscaling Group instances on Monday and reduce them on Friday as needed is inefficient and error-prone, as it requires continuous human intervention, and defeats the purpose of automation. Use dynamic scaling rules for scaling up at the start of the week and down at the end depends on metric-based triggers, not on known schedules, and may not respond in time for scheduled peaks. Set up a scheduled policy to scale out every Friday and scale in on Monday has the scaling actions in the wrong order, resulting in resources being available at incorrect times.

Tip: Scheduled scaling is best for predictable, time-based scaling events, while dynamic scaling is preferred for unpredictable traffic spikes.
