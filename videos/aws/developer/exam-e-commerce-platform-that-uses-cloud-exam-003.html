The correct option is Enable Cloud SQL high availability and configure automatic failover to a standby in another zone.

This configuration creates a regional Cloud SQL instance with a primary in one zone and a synchronous standby in another zone. If the primary zone experiences a failure then Cloud SQL automatically fails over to the standby so the service continues to accept traffic. During planned maintenance the instance can fail over to minimize disruption which helps meet the requirement to keep the billing and orders database available.

Create a cross-region read replica and plan to promote it during an incident is not suitable for high availability within a region because promotion is a manual operation and causes downtime. Read replicas are designed for read scaling and disaster recovery and they do not provide automatic failover for maintenance or zonal outages.

Provision two single-zone Cloud SQL instances in separate zones and keep them synchronized with custom scripts is not supported as a high availability pattern in Cloud SQL. You would be responsible for replication, health checks, and failover which risks data loss and split brain and does not provide the managed automatic failover the scenario requires.

Depend on automated backups and restore the database to a new instance in another zone during an outage leads to long recovery times and potential data loss. Restores are for backup and recovery and not for maintaining continuous service during maintenance or a zone failure.
